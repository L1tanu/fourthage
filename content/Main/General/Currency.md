Most places still use the imperial Marathan minted coinage as a form of currency following its fall with the exclusion of the copper coin which were all but lost due to being melted into tools as peasantry lacked a means of otherwise purchasing them. All but the Coral Mark can be split into smaller pieces; silver Moons split into ten Arcs, and golden Suns split into ten Spears. The following chart describes the conversions between coins:
- 10 Coral Marks = 1 Moon’s Arc
- 10 Moon’s Arcs = 1 Full Moon
- 10 Full Moons = 1 Sun’s Spear
- 10 Sun’s Spear = 1 Full Sun
