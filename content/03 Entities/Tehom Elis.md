## **Tehom Elis: Goddess of Vermin, Mutation, and Maternity.**
**Aliases:** The Primordial Mother, the Mother of Small Things, the Chimera. 
**Symbols:** A twin-tailed snake, chimeric Gallu, carrion birds, insects, rodents, the moon, mothers. 
**Worship:** Embrace chaos as life’s only constant; nurture the unloved, the unwanted, be a sanctuary for filth; shed your skin when the old becomes an irritant, never stop growing. 
**Dwelling:** The abyssal waters between worlds, the reflection of the moon, mirrors. 
**Equivalents:** Apasu (Orthian Heresy), folk aspect of Syah’varfutl (Aardayni), adversary to the Messiah (Virtudom).

Tehom Elis is depicted differently across cosmologies, but trends towards an anthropomorphic representation of the qualities men admire: beauty, mutability, mystery, and maternity. These are, most critically, always contrasted against monstrous or bestial features. To the Orthian Concord she is Apasu, a heretical interpretation of the Nadiral Exarch--serpentine sister to Concordia, his Dyad--who mingled with the causal waters to birth the lesser Exarchs. In Virtudom, she is a nymph and a temptress, adversary to the Messiah who seduced men and fashioned them into devils to stall his ascent. The schoolmen of Ansium viewed her as a shadow of Ista, and believed she birthed Athari from her union with Varasha. And in Aardayn, she is a folk aspect of Syah’varfutl who molded humanity from clay stained in the blood of Harm’lyoth. 

Today, Tehom Elis is worshipped in cults dispersed across Yiegdra who refer to her as the Mother of Small Things or the Primordial Mother. They believe her to be the origin of Gallu and an agent of radical metamorphoses-- nurturing the unwanted, revealing their true potential. She takes a serpentine effect, dwells in abyssal waters, and is only visible in the reflection of the moon. Her favored creatures are unloved by others: serpents, insects, carrion birds and vermin.

# **Acts of Favor**
- A bowl of dried insects or the meat of small creatures (1 Favor)
- A bowl of live insects (2 Favor)
- A live mouse, rat, crow or snake (2 Favor)
- The meat of a large beast or monster. (3 Favor)
- A live beast that is larger than a human (10 Favor)
- A live monster (25 Favor)
# **Invocations**
**Mastery over Small Things** Complexity: 1 Favor: 1 Range: Earshot Duration: Persist (6) AP: 1 Effect: You command a small animal or insect to do your bidding. If the creature is not hostile towards you, it will comply to the best of its ability. If it is hostile, it suffers Dazed (1). 

**Locust Swarm** Complexity: 2 Favor: 3 Range: Paces (5) Duration: Persist (1) AP: 2 Effect: Targets within Sphere (3) suffer the Blinded (1) and Unsteady (1) conditions. 

**Flock of Crows** Complexity: 3 Favor: 5 Range: Paces (5) Duration: Instant AP: 3 Effect: Select one target within the range. They must succeed a Willpower vs. Dexterity contest; on a failure, deepen all Minor wounds up to Moderate. 

**Fashioned from Fragments** Complexity: 4 Favor: 10 Range: Touch Duration: 1 Week AP: 5 Effect: You resurrect an inhuman corpse as an animalistic Gallu. It retains its Strength, halves its Dexterity and Willpower, and becomes a Hireling that does not require rations and cannot be demoralized. Living Hirelings are forced to make a morale save with Disadvantage (1) or immediately leave your service.
