Conditions can be applied by the environment, equipment, spells, or other sources.
- Environmental conditions end the instant you leave the area causing them (e.g., stepping out of the light of a torch, surfacing from water).
- Targeted conditions (spells, contraptions, etc.) end on the target's turn after Persist (#) expires or for AOEs, at the end of the turn in which you exited the area.
## Partially Covered
*behind a short wall, or a lattice fence* 
You have Advantage (1) on Contested defender rolls.
## Fully Covered
*behind a full stone wall, or a shut door*
You roll a flat 20 (not a Crit.) on Contested defender rolls.
## Unsteady
*fighting on a ship, wading through mud*
You cannot Block, Counter, or Evade.
## Slowed
*prone, navigating unfavorable terrain, physically impaired*
Your Speed is halved.
## Cramped
 *narrow halls or small caves*
 You have Disadvantage on any attacks made with weapons that have the [[05 Equipment#^1aedc4|Heavy]] or [[05 Equipment#^2fcf2e|Reach]] property. This is doubled if both apply.
## Burning
*engulfed in flame or boiling pitch*
 You gain 1 [[03 Encumbrance#^7b80a0|Minor Wound]] at the end of each of your turn. This clears all Freezing when applied. ^f44dc0
## Freezing
*chilled by frigid winds or water*
 Your maximum [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]] is reduced by 1. This clears all Burning when applied.^f2551e
## Bleeding (#)
*deep gouges or cut arteries*
You take # damage at the end of each of your turns. Stacking Bleeding adds to the existing #, and Persist resets to the longest duration.
## Falling
*falling from a steep cliff*
You move 100 Paces and take 1d6 Bludgeon for each 10 Paces fallen upon landing.
## Tethered (#)
*wrestling with another or tied to a post*
You cannot move further than # paces away from the source. ^6dd9a3
## Stuck
*manacled or trapped in brambles*
Your AP costs to Move, Pivot, and Evade are increased by 1.
## Dazed
*concussed or ringing ears*
You regain 1 [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]] less at the start of your turn.
## Blinded (#)
*smoke in the eyes, darkness, or a simple malformation*
You have Disadvantage (1) on Contests and Saves against sources beyond # paces, increasing by 1 for every additional # paces beyond that.
## Deprived
*sickness or starvation*
You cannot clear your [[03 Encumbrance#^795436|Fatigue]].
## Suffocating
*drowning or choking*
You can hold your breath for an amount of turns equal to your current Courage [[00 Characters#^50f785|Skill Score]], when you exceed that you gain the Suffocating status condition until you can safely breath again. You gain one [[03 Encumbrance#^795436|Fatigue]] at the end of each of your turns.
## Unconscious
*a forced reprieve*
You are unconscious. You cannot gain [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]].
## Dying
*the end*
You are dying. You cannot gain [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]] and gain a Minor Wound at the end of each of your turns. If you cannot gain a Minor Wound at the end of your turn, you die.
