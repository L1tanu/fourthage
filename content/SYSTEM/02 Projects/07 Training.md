When a character is called to a new vocation, they may spend time learning the necessary talents. As a Project, a character can study to remove their Skills and Abilities and regain the spent EXP points; alternatively, they may train one of their Hirelings in a Skill or Ability which they already possess. 

The DC is 10 with a number of Segments equal to the EXP being regained by a player, or the EXP cost of the Skill being taught to a Hireling. If attempting to teach a group of multiple Hirelings, the Segments increase by 1 per Hireling. If a player is regaining EXP with the intention of learning a Skill or Ability that another human Training participant has acquired (i.e. if they are being trained in Vigilance by a Hireling, or Invocations I-V by a player) then the DC is reduced to 5.
