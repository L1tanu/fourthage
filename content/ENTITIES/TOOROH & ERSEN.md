# Tööröh: Demon of Delirium, Obscurity, and Disappearances.

**Aliases:** The Forgetful, The Beast Beyond Borders
**Symbols:** Eyes in the night, ruined structures, empty beds, wild animals, fog.
**Worship:** Hide what you cherish, trust only in what you can see, protect the wild places, practice ignorance with purpose.
**Dwelling:** Southern Orralune, the marshes of Namia, and anywhere forgotten by man.
**Equivalents:** Son of the Exarchs Mendacium and Odium (Orthian Concord), Enemy of the Messiah (Virtudom), Aspect of Athari (Marathan Heartlands)

In the lands which surround modern Orralune, Tööröh is known as a treacherous and fickle demon. Mention of its name predates Tousale’s cooperation with the rebellion of Argal, and to this day many nomadic societies of the region still bow before its hidden shrines. It jealously guards the secret places of this world, often depicted in myth as the force which dooms the ventures of would-be pioneers or daring adventurers. Those who earn its ire must learn that not all things in this world were meant for man, or be doomed to disappear into that which they sought. The madness which can be brought on by isolation in the wilderness is often attributed to his presence, with many folk tales suggesting that it is his wish that mankind might lose itself entirely to its lesser, more animal nature.

Tööröh is oftentimes shown to exist without physical form, drawn as a pair of leering, bestial eyes which hang over its unwitting victims. When it is assigned a form, it is always that of something between man and beast: a wolf which walks with the too-knowing eyes of a human, or a stag whose gait carries it on two legs. It is thought to tirelessly stalk its prey without care for any barrier or border, speaking only to those who are already doomed to disappear alongside it.

# Ersën: Demon of Curiosity, Discovery, and the Hubris of Man.

**Aliases:** The Forager, An Uncertainty Before Sleep, Disaster’s Architect.
**Symbols:** A brightly hooded man, owls, archways, migratory birds, and flutes.
**Worship:** Leave no rock unturned, no path unwalked, nor solution unconsidered. Travel far and wide, acting as guides to others behind you. Find his hidden signs and forever evade the beast.
**Dwelling:** Southern Orralune, the marshes of Namia, and any path walked by man.
**Equivalents:** The Wind Riding Wren (Aardayni), a trickster spirit (Virtudom), Aspect of Kutuhali (Marathan Heartlands)

Ever present where the name Tööröh curses the lands around modern Orralune, Ersën is known as a trickster spirit who offers insight to man without thought for its consequences. It is endlessly hunted by its prowling counterpart, cast in folk tales as the lure which first tempts man to trespass in the world’s hidden places. While some stories aggrandize the secret paths it has revealed to man, or the innovations found at their ends, many more warn against the folly its gifts bring about. Worship of the forager is undertaken both in supplication, and the hope that one’s own discoveries might amuse the spirit for long enough that it does not think to lead you astray. It serves as patron and protector to several nomadic peoples of the empire’s western front, offered worship in exchange for its support of their endless migration.

Depictions of Ersën invariably involve the presence of a bird, usually an owl or a swallow. They either perch on the shoulder of a faceless, hooded man, or are themselves the inhabitants of the cloak: countless beating wings and glassy eyes bound by cloth in the shape of a man. These birds are the only means by which the spirit is known to communicate, chirping in the ears of its subjects.
