# Session 0
Baron is sending them to a small hamlet set up around an iron mine that acts as housing for the miners.
- Short trip into the mountains, find a small caravan that was diverted away from the usual cartway with the shipment returned.
- To investigate the situation there and figure out how to restart the process
	- To retrieve the "baron's" family sword once they can investigate this a bit further

The banditry is being funded by Marathan forces intending to destabilize the region so they can supplant it. The 'cause' is being spear headed by the original baron's son, but is struggling to regain a foothold in the region. Those with the baron's son are only doing as with the funds and supplies being sent over as they have no love for the previous family, but remained imprisoned due to their dislike of the current baron after they failed to support his coup.

Mining hamlet was up in arms previously about the quality of timber they were being supplied with as previously there had been several collapses due to them being forced to space them too far apart or having to use subpar wood. Due to this, the old baron's son (Lugh Grufydd) thought he could use this to his advantage, but it blew up in his face as most people rejected his claims to leadership. He woke one of the ancient Gates (poorly) that was previously used in the bloomery, and cast its fire about the hamlet, destroying it. The event will culminate in them making it to the mine and attempting to drive him out of it. Below there are wretches made of the workers and the man himself, marred by his actions and attempts to refashion this Gate.

What are the players going to be doing?

	2. The cart's pace increased as they realized that the hamlet was burning and a set of footsteps is seen catching up to them as the few workers from other sites joined in on the banditry.
