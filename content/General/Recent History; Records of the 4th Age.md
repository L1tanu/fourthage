Below is a bullet point list of important events that occurred within the past 50 years. The current year is 4A 128.
## **THE PAST 10 YEARS:**
- Armistice ending the Century of Skirmishes: 4 A 123
	Peace is brokered between Lagain and Tousav, ending the Century of Skirmishes, which is a series of conflicts that began in 4A 9. The terms of this treaty are hidden to the wider public; only the highest officials within the Empire and the Holy Kingdom know.

- War of Gwen: Tousavi Invasion of Gwen: 4 A 121
	The Empire invades the land of Gwen, Lagainian territory on the eastern frontier. Within months the forces of Gwen are exhausted, and an Imperial conquest of Gwen seemed likely.

- Reconquest of Manster: 4 A 119
	The end to the Inenkinite Rebellion in Lagain and the dissolution of the Aigéanach Alliance after months of fighting across the eastern seaboard. The deciding factor was the return of the Holy Kingdom's army from the southern warfront, ordered by Immortal Allelaeon. Duke Nemain IV, Alliance Commander, is stripped of his title and publicly executed alongside his co-conspirators in Caledon. A blood tithe is instituted, and children within the Inenkinite territories are taken to "replace the sevisars lost quelling the rebellion."
## **THE PAST 20 YEARS:**
- Vardagh’s Conversion to Lagain: 4 A 106
	After witnessing the destruction of the Tyric and Sedalic lands by the invading forces of Tousav, Vardagh assimilates into the Holy Kingdom as part of a protection agreement. This is credited to the work of Lagainian missionaries, who had been working in Sedalic lands for years.

- The Massacre of Glamis Rock: 4 A 106
	The massacre of the capital of Eilidh at the hands of the Imperial army. This event was organized by Vainrence Blair, the “Thunder King," who was the Duke of Semvar and a double-agent working for the Empire. The mass death resulted in the surrender of the Eilidhese and the official reorganization of their lands as Littoire, ruled by the Northern Seneschal.

- Beginning of the Inenkinite Rebellion: 4 A 103
	In response to increasing persecution from the Orthian Concord, the Inenkinite lands of Lagain--Manster, Fenia, and Lochia--form the Aigéanach Alliance and rebel. This revolution was led by the Duke of Manster, Nemain IV.

- Massacre at Ormond: Tousav's invasion of Eichencourt and Yethselune: 4 A 102
	Imperial push into Lagain's southern border territories, successfully occupying them after years of fighting in Montclaive. The height of these battles was the Massacre at Ormond, a city in Eichencourt bordering Altrecht. These territories are integrated into Montclaive under the rule of the Eastern Seneschal.

- Tousav's invasion of Prisca and Ursia: 4 A 100
	Hiring foreign mercenaries from south of the Gray Marches, Tousav launches an invasion of Prisca and Ursia from the lands of Orralune with the intention of connecting the Empire to the Yiegdran sea and creating a new province under the rule of a Seneschal.
