# Diseases
There are many perils a character will face throughout their lifetime--some more slow and insidious. When a character encounters a source of infection, they must roll a [[01 Combat#^40aef1|Save]] to avoid contracting a Disease. If they fail, they become infected.

Once a character has been infected by a Disease, it will enter gestation. Each Disease has a different gestation length, ranging from several days to several weeks, at the end of which the infected character must repeat the [[01 Combat#^40aef1|Save]]. Depending on their roll, the Disease may worsen or recover. The severity of a Disease is measured in stages. Each stage comes with intensifying debuffs and symptoms.

The Disease progresses based on the [[01 Combat#^40aef1|Save]] result:
- Catastrophic (below DC by 5 or more; disease progresses two stages)
- Failure (below DC; disease progresses one stage)
- Success (equal, or above DC; disease does not progress)
- Breakthrough (above DC by 5 or more; character is cured)

A player can avoid rolling their [[01 Combat#^40aef1|Save]] by undertaking a [[00 Projects#^88e11f|Recovery Project]] and using Medicine (#) equal to the Disease’s stage. At the end of the  [[00 Projects#^88e11f|Recovery]] , they are automatically cured.

Some examples of common Diseases are:
## Rashes
Transmission: Bacterial infection, contact with poison, contact with the infected.
Save: Might (DC 11)
Gestation: 3 days; appearance of itchy, inflamed rashes across body.
- Stage Increase (1+ week): Above symptom worsens.
- The infected counts as having 1 less DR.
## Digestive Issues
Transmission: Consuming rotten or uncooked food, drinking unclean water.
Save: Might (DC 14)
Gestation: 3 days; stomach pain, nausea, vomiting, spasm of the entrails.
- Stage Increase (1+ week): The symptoms worsen.
- The infected restores 1 less Fatigue when resting.
## Infected Wound
Transmission: Leaving Minor, Moderate, or Major wounds untreated; resting with wounds.
Save: Might (DC 14)
Gestation: 3 days; pus, reddening around wound, intensifying pain, darkening veins.
- Stage Increase (3+ days): The symptoms worsen.
- The infected wound deepens. If the wound is Major, its Overkill increases by 1 stage.
