# Conditions
Conditions can be applied by the environment, equipment, spells, or a myriad of other things. Conditions applied by the environment immediately end when you are no longer in the area applying them (ie, lighting a torch in a pitch black or lifting your head out of water.) Conditions applied by other effects (ie, targeted magical effects or a poison) explain how they are applied or removed.
## Partially Covered
(behind a short wall, or a lattice fence): 
Advantage on Contested defender rolls and +5 to Saves.
## Fully Covered
(behind a full stone wall, or a shut door)
Flat 20 (not a Crit.) on Contested defender rolls and +10 to Saves.
## Unsteady (#)
(fighting on a ship, wading through mud)
You cannot take the [[01 Combat#^da09d9|Evade]] reaction, then reduce # by 1 at the end of your turn.
## Prone
Movement costs twice as much.
## Cramped
 (narrow halls or small caves)
 Disadvantage on any attacks made with weapons that have the [[05 Equipment#^1aedc4|Heavy]] or [[05 Equipment#^2fcf2e|Reach]] property. This is doubled if both apply.
## Burning (#)
 You gain 1 [[03 Encumbrance#^7b80a0|Minor Wound]] at the end of each of your turns, then reduce # by 1. Reduces [[04 Conditions#^f2551e|Freezing]] by #. ^f44dc0
## Freezing (#)
 Your maximum [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]] is reduced by #, then reduce # by 1. Reduces [[04 Conditions#^f44dc0|Burning]] by #. ^f2551e
## Bleeding (#)
You take # untyped damage at the end of each of your turns, then reduce # by 1.
## Falling
You move 100 Paces and take 1d6 Bludgeon for each 10 Paces fallen upon landing.
## Tethered (#)
You cannot move further than # paces away from the source. ^6dd9a3
## Stuck
You cannot take the Pivot action or change Facing.
## Dazed (#)
You regain 1 [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]] less at the start of your turn, then reduce # by 1.
## Healing (#)
You regain # HP at the end of your turn, then reduce # by 1.
## Blinded (#)
You have Disadvantage (1) on Contests and Saves against sources outside your Blinded (#). ^df7b65
## Deprived
You cannot clear your [[03 Encumbrance#^795436|Fatigue]].
## Suffocating
You can hold your breath for an amount of turns equal to your current Courage [[00 Characters#^50f785|Skill Score]], when you exceed that you gain the Suffocating status condition until you can safely breath again. You gain one [[03 Encumbrance#^795436|Fatigue]] at the end of each of your turns.
## Dying
You are dying. You cannot gain [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]] and gain a Minor Wound at the end of each of your turns. If you cannot gain a Minor Wound at the end of your turn, you die.
## Unconscious
You are unconscious. You cannot gain [[01 Combat#^72491d|AP]].
